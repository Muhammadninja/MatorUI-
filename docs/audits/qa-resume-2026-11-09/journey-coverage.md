## How to read the evidence

- **Tests** links identify existing assertions inspected in source. Their existence does not mean they passed on this commit; no tests were rerun for this documentation change.
- **Historical evidence** keeps its original source, platform and data scope. [Post-fix results](index.md#root-cause-confirmed-and-fix-verified-claude-resume-2026-11-09) record two passing flow-12/13 pairs on source `41ab8a3`, APK `a68b3474…`, Android API35, EN/light, loopback mock. They do not verify the requested combined APK.
- **Automation acceptance** is [locally and in CI verified](candidate-build/provenance.md). Flow-12/13 use loopback mock data and do not verify the deployed backend.
- **Missing** means a test or acceptance artifact was not found in this review, or the cited source explicitly leaves the check open. It does not prove the behavior is broken.
- Source labels distinguish explicit owner instructions, documented contracts, historical issues and behavior inferred from implementation/tests. Historical documents do not establish the current deployed backend version.
