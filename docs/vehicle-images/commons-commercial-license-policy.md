# Wikimedia Commons commercial image policy

The vehicle image pipeline accepts Commons image metadata only when Commons supplies an explicit license URL that maps exactly to this allowlist:

- CC0 1.0
- Creative Commons Attribution 2.0, 2.5, 3.0, or 4.0
- Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 2.0, 2.5, 3.0, or 4.0

The pipeline rejects missing or unrecognized license evidence, NonCommercial licenses, NoDerivatives licenses, GFDL, and other licenses that are not explicitly listed. A license label without its matching canonical license URL, author, attribution, source page, and original HTTPS image URL is insufficient for approval.

Commons images remain private Admin Review candidates. Human approval must confirm the image shows the exact vehicle identity and generation, the visible image has no watermark, and the license and attribution remain accurate. The reviewer records the rights evidence they checked. Automatic publication is always disabled for Wikimedia.

Photos are decoded and resized without cropping or removing their background, then stored as WebP. The Garage response presents the photographer credit, license, source page, and the resize/format modification notice. CC BY-SA derivatives retain their source license and attribution metadata.
